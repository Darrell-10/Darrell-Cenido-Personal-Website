Personal Statement

Navigating Life's Maze

Faith, creativity, family, basketball, travel, and computer science — each one a different path through the same labyrinth.

Instagram LinkedIn Email

Life is like a complex maze, a labyrinth of twists and turns that challenge our determination and test our resilience. From my faith, to my love for computer science, from enchanting garden mazes to family adventures in local corn mazes, each of these aspects in my life have contributed to my ability to navigate the metaphorical maze called life.

My journey through this maze began with my Kairos retreat, a transformative experience that deepened my understanding of faith and formed lasting bonds with my peers. In the calm setting of the retreat, there was a maze made out of stones and dirt. There, I connected with my brothers through meaningful conversations and reflection. Like fractal mazes, each of us are part of a bigger community. Faith and relationships became my compass.

Life is also like the video game Labyrinth. Each level mirrors real-world problems. Strategizing through that digital maze taught me resilience and problem-solving — skills essential in technology. Games like these helped me realize I want to create and follow the path of computer science.

Travel added another piece. Wandering the maze at the gardens of Versailles reinforced my belief that life's most rewarding moments often come from curiosity and navigating uncharted territories. Basketball, part of my life since age four, remains my “blackout maze” — every section visited, every cell explored — and now a craft I share while coaching local CYO teams.

Like a weave maze, my family is the undying support guiding me through challenges. Annual cruise trips have woven our bonds tighter. I embrace the metaphorical maze of life, knowing that through these experiences and passions I continue to navigate, grow, and thrive.
